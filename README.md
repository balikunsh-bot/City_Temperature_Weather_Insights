## 1. Comparative City Analysis (2020–2024)

Our study analyzed five cities to identify climate patterns and risks.

### Key Findings

- **Temperature Trends**: All cities show a consistent, slight upward trend in annual average temperatures.
- **Extremes**:
  - **Delhi** recorded the highest average temperature: **30.7°C**
  - **Mumbai** recorded:
    - Highest rainfall (up to **3600 mm**)  
    - Highest humidity (up to **91%**)  
  - **Bengaluru** remained the coolest (26.2°C → 26.8°C)
- **Statistical Correlations**:
  - Strong correlation (**0.903**) between humidity and rainfall  
  - Weak correlation (**0.177**) between temperature and rainfall  

### Dataset Summary

| City | Climate Classification | Notable Peak Year |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Bengaluru** | Moderate Rainfall | 2024 (Hottest & Wettest) |
| **Delhi** | Low Rainfall / Dry | 2024 (Hottest) |
| **Mumbai** | Very High Rainfall / Coastal | 2024 (Hottest & Wettest) |

## 2. Bengaluru Crisis Overview

Bengaluru is experiencing increasing climate volatility:

- **Irregular Rainfall** → Sudden intense downpours  
- **Rising Heat** → More frequent hotter years  
- **Impact**:
  - Water security issues  
  - Heat-related health risks  
  - Urban flooding & sewage overflow

## 3. Proposed Solution: Smart Climate Response System

We propose a data-driven system to help cities anticipate and respond to climate events.

### Framework

1. **Monitor** – IoT sensors tracking live rainfall, temperature, humidity, pressure  
2. **Predict** – Machine learning models forecasting:
   - Flood-risk zones  
   - Heavy rainfall days  
   - Heatwave probability  
3. **Alert** – Auto-notifications via SMS/WhatsApp  
4. **Act** – Data-driven actions:
   - Drainage cleaning  
   - Water storage optimization  
   - Traffic redirection  

### Expected Impact

- **Reduced Water Stress** via efficient distribution & harvesting  
- **Resilience** through early-warning systems  
- **Heat Control** using cool roofing and green cover expansion
