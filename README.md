## 1. Comparative City Analysis (2020–2024)

Our study analyzed five cities to identify climate patterns and risks.

### Key Findings

- **Temperature Trends**: All cities show a steady upward trend in annual average temperatures.
- **Extremes**:
  - **Delhi** recorded the highest average temperature: **30.7°C**
  - **Mumbai** recorded:
    - Highest rainfall (up to **3600 mm**)  
    - Highest humidity (up to **91%**)  
  - **Bengaluru** remained the coolest city (26.2°C → 26.8°C)
- **Statistical Correlations**:
  - Strong correlation (**0.903**) between humidity & rainfall  
  - Weak correlation (**0.177**) between temperature & rainfall  

### Dataset Summary

| City        | Climate Classification      | Notable Peak Year         |
|------------|------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Bengaluru** | Moderate Rainfall            | 2024 (Hottest & Wettest)  |
| **Delhi**     | Low Rainfall / Dry           | 2024 (Hottest)            |
| **Mumbai**    | Very High Rainfall / Coastal | 2024 (Hottest & Wettest)  |

## 2. Bengaluru Crisis Overview

Bengaluru is experiencing increasing climate instability:

- **Irregular Rainfall** → Sudden intense downpours  
- **Rising Heat** → Frequent hotter years  
- **Impacts**:
  - Water scarcity  
  - Heat-related health issues  
  - Urban flooding & sewage overflow

## 3. Proposed Solution: Smart Climate Response System

A technology-driven framework designed to improve urban climate resilience.

### Framework

1. **Monitor** – IoT sensors tracking temperature, humidity, rainfall, pressure  
2. **Predict** – ML models forecasting:
   - Flood-risk zones  
   - Heavy rainfall days  
   - Heatwave probability  
3. **Alert** – Auto notifications via SMS/WhatsApp  
4. **Act** – Data-driven municipal actions:
   - Drainage cleaning  
   - Water storage optimization  
   - Traffic redirection  

### Expected Impact

- **Reduced Water Stress**  
- **Improved Climate Resilience**  
- **Heat Control** through cool roofs & increased green cover
